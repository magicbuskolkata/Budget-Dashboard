Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 21 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Budget Monitoring Dashboard ("the Dashboard", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Dashboard.

1. About the Dashboard

Budget Monitoring Dashboard is a browser-based reporting and budget monitoring application designed to display and analyse budget and financial information stored in authorised Google Drive files.

2. Google Account Access

The Dashboard may use Google authentication to allow an authorised user to connect their Google Account and access the Google Drive data required for the Dashboard to operate.

The Dashboard requests only the Google permissions required for its intended functionality.

3. Google Drive Data

When you authorise Google Drive access, the Dashboard may access files that are necessary to provide its budget monitoring and reporting functionality.

The Dashboard does not intentionally modify, delete, or share your Google Drive files.

4. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the Dashboard is used only for displaying, analysing, filtering, and reporting budget and financial information within the Dashboard.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information obtained through the Dashboard.

5. Data Storage

The Dashboard is primarily a browser-based application. Information accessed from Google Drive is processed for the purpose of displaying the requested information to the authorised user.

Unless specifically stated otherwise, the Dashboard does not maintain a separate database containing copies of users' Google Drive files.

6. Google API Services

The Dashboard uses Google API Services to authenticate users and access authorised Google Drive information.

Use of Google API Services is subject to Google's policies and terms.

You can review Google's privacy practices here: Google Privacy Policy

7. Information Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect access to the Dashboard. Users should also ensure that their Google Account is protected with appropriate security measures, including a strong password and, where available, two-step verification.

8. Third-Party Services

The Dashboard may rely on services provided by Google, including Google Authentication, Google Drive, and Google APIs.

These services operate under their respective terms and privacy policies.

9. User Control

You can control access granted to applications through your Google Account settings. You may revoke the Dashboard's Google access at any time through your Google Account.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the Dashboard, applicable requirements, or data handling practices.
